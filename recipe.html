Spaghetti cacio e pepe

Three ingredients, one pan and a little patience. The trick is the starchy water, not cream.

By Cris · Updated 28 October 2026 · Leave a comment

Photo: finished cacio e pepe, landscape 3:2
  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Serves 2

Jump to recipe

Ingredients

2 servings
  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 100 g pecorino romano, finely grated
  • 2 tsp black peppercorns, freshly cracked
  • Salt for the pasta water

Kitchen kit for this recipe: a wide stainless pan and a pepper mill. Affiliate links

Method

  1. Toast the pepper in a dry, wide pan for 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. Boil the spaghetti in lightly salted water. Keep a mug of the starchy cooking water.
  3. Stir the pecorino with a few spoons of warm pasta water into a thick paste.
  4. Move the pasta to the pepper pan with a splash of water. Take it off the heat, add the paste and stir until glossy.

Cris’s tip

If it clumps, the pan was too hot. Add a spoon of warm water and keep stirring away from the heat.
